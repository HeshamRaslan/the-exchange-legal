Privacy Policy

The Exchange · Effective May 9, 2026

Quick summary. The Exchange is a single-player idle game. We collect the minimum data needed to run the game, save your progress, show you ads (unless you've paid to remove them), and process in-app purchases through Apple and Google. We do not sell your data.

1. Who we are

"The Exchange" (the "Game") is developed and operated by Hesham Raslan, an independent solo developer ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you play, why we collect it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email hesham_raslann@hotmail.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

You can play The Exchange entirely without giving us any personal information. If you choose to register an account (to enable cloud save, leaderboards, or referrals), we collect:

  • Email address — only if you sign in with email, or with Apple / Google and choose to share your email.
  • Display name — a username you pick for the leaderboard. You can use a pseudonym.
  • Optional profile information — for example a short bio, if you choose to add one in a future Verified-tier feature.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you play, we and our service providers automatically collect:

  • Device information — device model, operating system, OS version, language, time zone, screen resolution.
  • Usage data — how you interact with the Game (sessions, features used, in-game events, crashes, errors).
  • Approximate location — country and region, derived from IP address. We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Identifiers — a Firebase anonymous user ID for save data and, on iOS only with your explicit permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt, the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). On Android, the Google Advertising ID may be used by our ad provider, subject to your device privacy settings.
  • Game progress — your portfolio, holdings, cash, prestige history, streak, trade history, settings, and other data stored in your save file. This is required for the Game to function.

2.3 Information from third parties

If you sign in with Apple or Google, those services share a unique account identifier and (if you allow it) your email and name with us. We never see your password.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Operate the Game and save your progress.
  • Sync your save data across devices if you create an account.
  • Show in-game leaderboards and (in future versions) optional social features such as friends and referrals.
  • Provide rewarded video ads through ironSource LevelPlay, unless you have purchased the "Remove Ads" / Premium Pass option.
  • Process in-app purchases (handled by Apple and Google, not by us).
  • Detect and prevent cheating, fraud, and abuse.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve the Game's stability and performance.
  • Send push notifications you have agreed to receive (you can disable these at any time in your device settings).
  • Respond to your support requests.
  • Comply with our legal obligations.

4. Legal basis (EEA / UK users)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on the following legal bases under the GDPR / UK GDPR:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the Game and the features you've requested.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the Game running, prevent abuse, and improve gameplay.
  • Consent — for tracking via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS and for marketing-style push notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable laws.

6. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, before any cross-app tracking can occur, you'll see Apple's standard App Tracking Transparency prompt asking whether to allow tracking. You can choose to allow or deny it. Denying does not affect your ability to play the Game. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to fix inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your account and associated data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection / restriction — ask us to stop or limit certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hesham_raslann@hotmail.com from the address associated with your account. We will respond within 30 days.

7.1 California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you also have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and share.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not sell personal information in the traditional sense, but our use of advertising identifiers may qualify as "sharing" under California law. You can opt out by denying the App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS or limiting ad tracking on Android.
  • Non-discrimination for exercising these rights.

8. Children's privacy

The Exchange is rated 17+ on the App Store and intended for users aged 17 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA / UK). If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Data retention

We retain your save data and account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Game. If you request deletion or stop playing, we will delete or anonymise your personal information within a reasonable time, except where we are required to keep it for legal or fraud-prevention reasons.

10. Data security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit (HTTPS / TLS) and access controls on our service providers' infrastructure (Google Firebase, Unity, etc.). No system is perfectly secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International data transfers

Our service providers (Google, Unity, Apple, ironSource) operate globally and may store and process data in countries outside your own, including in the United States. Where required, we and our providers rely on lawful data transfer mechanisms such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Effective" date at the top and, where appropriate, notify you in the Game or by other reasonable means. The current version is always available at heshamraslan.github.io/the-exchange-legal/privacy.html.
