About The Exchange

What is The Exchange?

The Exchange is an idle mobile game where you start with $1,000 and build a fictional investment empire by buying stocks, collecting dividends, surviving market crashes, and prestige-resetting to grow exponentially stronger. Along the way, a mentor character named Kash teaches real-world investing concepts in plain English.

Is this real investing? Can I make real money?

No. The Exchange is a fictional simulation for entertainment only. All companies, stocks, prices, and market events are imaginary. Strategies that work in the Game may fail (or be illegal) in real markets. Nothing in the Game is financial advice. Always consult a licensed financial professional before making real investment decisions.

Saves and progress

I lost my progress / how do I save my progress?

By default, your progress is saved locally on the device you're playing on. To enable cloud save (so your progress follows you between devices and survives an uninstall), open the Profile tab and sign in with Apple, Google, or email. Once signed in, your save is backed up to our servers automatically every time you exit the game.

If you uninstalled the Game without signing in, your local save was deleted with the app — we have no way to recover it. We strongly recommend signing in once you're past the first cycle.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple, Google, or email account on the new phone. Your save will download from the cloud automatically on first launch. If you have an active save on the new device that you don't want, the Game will ask whether to keep the local save or load the cloud save before overwriting anything.

I want to delete my account and data

Email hesham_raslann@hotmail.com from the address associated with your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your save data, account record, and any associated personal information within 30 days. This is permanent and cannot be undone. See the Privacy Policy for full details on your data rights.

Purchases and subscriptions

How do I get a refund?

All purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play, so refund requests need to go through them directly — we are not able to issue refunds ourselves.

  • iOS: Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find the purchase, and request a refund.
  • Android: Open Google Play, go to your Order history, find the purchase, and tap "Request a refund".
How do I cancel my VIP subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by your app store, not by us:

  • iOS: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → The Exchange → Cancel.
  • Android: Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → The Exchange → Cancel.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period — you keep VIP perks until then.

I bought something and didn't get it

Force-close and reopen the Game. The store will retry delivery on launch. If the purchase still hasn't arrived after a restart, in the Profile tab tap "Restore Purchases" — this re-checks your store entitlements. If neither works, email us with your receipt or order number from the App Store / Play Store and we'll investigate.

My promo code isn't working

Codes are case-sensitive — type it exactly as shown, with the same letter case. Common reasons a valid-looking code is rejected:

  • The code has expired or reached its redemption limit.
  • You've already redeemed this code on this account.
  • The code has restrictions (e.g. VIP-only, certain prestige tier).
  • You're trying too many codes too quickly — wait an hour and try again.

If you're sure the code should work, email us with the code and your in-game username and we'll check.

Gameplay

My streak broke — can I get it back?

Streaks broken within the last 24 hours can be restored using a Streak Freeze. You get one free freeze every Monday, and VIP players have a bank of up to 7. If you're out of freezes, you can spend 15 Golden Shares for an emergency retroactive freeze (within 24 hours of missing a day). After 24 hours have passed, the streak cannot be restored.

What counts as a "qualifying action" for keeping my streak?

Any of these:

  • Buying or selling a stock (market or limit order).
  • Setting or modifying a limit, stop-loss, or take-profit order.
  • Completing an Academy lesson.
  • Responding to a player decision popup during a Rare or Breaking event.

Just opening the app does not count. Make at least one trade per day.

I went bankrupt — what now?

If your total portfolio drops below $150, a rescue card appears on the Portfolio tab. You can:

  • Use one free rescue per cycle (resets to $1,000 cash).
  • Watch a rewarded ad for a second rescue in the same cycle.
  • Spend 50 Golden Shares for a premium rescue ($10,000 cash) — always available, doesn't consume your free-rescue counter.

Bugs and crashes

The Game crashed / my save looks wrong

Force-close and reopen first — most one-off issues clear up on a fresh launch. If the issue persists, email us with:

  • What you were doing when it happened
  • Your device model and OS version (e.g. "iPhone 14, iOS 17.2")
  • Your in-game username (if you have one)
  • A screenshot if it's a visual issue

Crash reports are also automatically collected (anonymously) by Firebase Crashlytics, so we may already have it logged on our side.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email hesham_raslann@hotmail.com with the subject "Bug" or "Feature request". Solo dev studio — every email is read personally.
